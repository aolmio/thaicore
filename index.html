🪞 Mid Class Mirror Anchor
Mid class: Ek→Low, Tho→Falling, Tri→High, Chattawa→Rising. Predictable every time.
🏴‍☠️ Low Class Rebel Shift Jump
Low class + marks jump one level higher: Mai Ek → Falling, Mai Tho → High.
💥 Dead Syllable Choke-Spike Pressure
Low + short dead → High ˊ. Long dead → Falling ˆ. The stop chokes pitch upward.
🍏 Baseline Checklist
Live: long vowel or nasal → Mid baseline.
Dead: stop or short vowel → Low or High by class.
🎵 ห Leading Elevator
ห before sonorant (ม น ง ว ล ย ร) → High class silently. หมา / หนาว / หวาน → Rising ˇ.
🔵 อ Leading Silencer
Silent อ before ย/ว → Mid class. อยู่ + Mai Ek → Low Tone.
🕳️ Invisible Vowel
Thai skips /o/ or /a/ between clusters. คน = kh+o+n. กระ = g+r+a.
📈 Pitch Shape Guide
Mid — flat · Low ˋ gentle descent · Falling ˆ peak then sharp drop · High ˊ rises & holds · Rising ˇ dips then climbs.
